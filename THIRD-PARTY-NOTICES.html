Rust crates

The crates ren is built from, among them Slint (used under the GNU GPL 3), Blitz, Stylo, Servo, Brave's adblock-rust, rustls, ureq, rusqlite with SQLite, and keyring, are listed with their licences in Cargo.lock and crates/ren-servo/Cargo.lock of ren's source; cargo deny check licenses checks them against the licences compatible with the GPL.
