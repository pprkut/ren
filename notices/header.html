Ren is built on the work of many free and open source projects. This page lists their copyright notices and licences: what isn't a Rust crate, the libraries inside crates, and the crates of Ren and its web page helper, generated with cargo-about (just notices). Among them are Slint (used under the GNU GPL 3), Blitz, Stylo, Servo with SpiderMonkey, Brave's adblock-rust, rustls, ureq, rusqlite with SQLite, and keyring.

The logo

Ren's logo, a wire-frame lotus, is Ren's own, under the GNU GPL 3 or later, like its code.
